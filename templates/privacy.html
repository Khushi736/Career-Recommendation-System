Privacy Policy

How SmartCareerAI handles your data

1. Introduction

At SmartCareerAI, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our platform and use our AI-driven career services. Please read this policy carefully.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when registering for an account and using our services. This includes:

  • Personal Data: Name, email address, and profile picture.
  • Professional Data: Resumes/CVs you upload, identified skills, career interests, and assessment scores.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with our ATS Checker, Courses, and Career Roadmap.

3. How We Use Your Data

Our primary use of your data is to provide and improve our services. Specifically, we use your information to:

  • Power our AI engine to generate your Personalized Career Roadmap.
  • Analyze your resume against ATS algorithms to provide optimization feedback.
  • Recommend curated courses based on identified skill gaps.
  • Create and manage your user account.

4. AI Processing and Data Storage

When you upload a resume or input skills, this data is processed by our artificial intelligence algorithms. We retain your profile data to provide a continuous, seamless experience. We do not sell your personal data or resume information to third-party data brokers.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to help protect your personal information. However, please be aware that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to request access to the personal data we collect from you, change that information, or delete it entirely. To request the deletion of your account and associated data, please contact our support team.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions or comments about this Privacy Policy, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@smartcareerai.com.
