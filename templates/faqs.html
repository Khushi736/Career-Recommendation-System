Everything you need to know about using the SmartCareerAI platform.

General & Account

SmartCareerAI is an AI-driven platform designed to empower your professional journey. We analyze your skills and interests to recommend ideal career paths, optimize your resume, and bridge knowledge gaps.

Currently, you are registered as a Free Member. You can view upgrade options and manage your subscription by opening the profile menu on the top right.

Core Features

On your main dashboard, simply type a skill (e.g., JavaScript) or interest into the respective text boxes and click the blue "+" button. To remove a tag, click the "x" next to its name. Once you are done, click "Apply Changes" to save your preferences.

Our AI performs a real-time analysis based on your profile inputs and uploaded CV. It then generates a roadmap, showing career titles alongside a Match Percentage to indicate how well your current profile aligns with that role.

Resume & Assessments

Navigate to the ATS Checker from the left-hand menu. Upload your resume, and our system will analyze it against common Applicant Tracking System algorithms to ensure your formatting and keywords are optimized for recruiters.

If our AI detects a gap between your current skills and your desired career path, we will recommend specific, curated Courses from our library to help you bridge that knowledge gap and improve your match percentage.
